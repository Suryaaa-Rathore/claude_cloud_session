# How a UPI payment works (60 s, 9:16): treatment

**Style:** Isometric Infographic (lemo-opuscar). **Voice:** an English narrator (Kokoro, offline). **Format:** 1080×1920, 60 fps.

## Three candidate structures
1. **The tracked subject.** One ₹ coin, with a "you are here" ring, rides the route from a phone, through its app bank, into the NPCI switch, to the payer's bank, then to the shop's bank and back as two confirmations. Clear cause and effect, and one continuous take.
2. **Two boards.** Cash versus UPI, side by side. Good contrast, but it spends half of 60 s on the old way.
3. **One icon multiplies.** It opens on a single payment, ends on 800 million a day, and leaves no time for the mechanism.

**Chosen: 1, ending with 3's scale reveal.** The diorama is a floating board shaped like a city block, with five stations:
- **YOU:** a phone on a café table;
- **APP:** the app's partner bank, a small tower;
- **NPCI UPI SWITCH:** a central exchange block with a cutaway;
- **YOUR BANK:** a tower with a vault;
- **SHOP'S BANK:** a tower, next to a chai stall with a QR stand.

Route lines draw slightly ahead of the request packet. The packet is an envelope carrying a lock icon, and its hue is reserved for it.
