# What should your brain eat? (Data Storytelling, 9:16, ~75 s)

**Decided by me:** the style is Data Storytelling. The topic is specific nutrition values, and this style makes every mark a real number, drawn by a pencil, with notes pinned to the values. Vertical 1080×1920 at 60 fps, English narrator, about 75 s.

## Three candidate structures
1. **The question first (chosen).** An empty plot with a handwritten question; five foods arrive one serving at a time, each as a bar against its daily target, with a pinned evidence note. The turn is the omega-3 chart, where the headline is revised: walnuts win by weight, but salmon wins on DHA once the bars morph. Ending: one plate, the five marks gathered.
2. **A ranked bar race of %DV.** Fails because the nutrients have no common unit (DHA and anthocyanins have no DV).
3. **One meal → a week.** A scale switch; too much for 75 s.
