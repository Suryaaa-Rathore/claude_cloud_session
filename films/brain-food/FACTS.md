# Facts (claim → source)
- Spinach, raw, 1 cup: vitamin K 145 mcg (144.87), 121% DV (DV 120 mcg). USDA FoodData Central via UH Hospitals nutrition facts; NIH ODS vitamin K table (not directly reachable here).
- Morris MC et al., Neurology 2018 (Rush Memory and Aging Project, n = 960, mean age 81, ~5 yrs): ~1 serving/day of green leafy vegetables associated with slower cognitive decline, "the equivalent of 11 years younger". Association, not causation.
- Egg, 1 large: choline 147 mg, 27% DV (DV 550 mg). NIH ODS choline table via PMC6259877 and others. Choline is the precursor of acetylcholine (standard biochemistry).
- Walnuts, English, 1 oz: ALA 2.57 g. Salmon, Atlantic, farmed, cooked, 3 oz: DHA 1.24 g, EPA 0.59 g (1.83 g EPA+DHA). NIH ODS omega-3 table (as quoted by secondary sources).
- ALA → DHA conversion generally under 1% (NIH-attributed summary, secondary source); so 2.57 g ALA → < 0.026 g DHA.
- DHA ≈ 15–20% of total fatty acids in the adult frontal cortex (PMC review).
- Blueberries: total anthocyanins ~123–182 mg/100 g in USDA-cited samples (Oregon State berry health fact sheet; Wu et al. 2006 J Agric Food Chem 54:4069); varies widely by cultivar (71–534 mg/100 g). Memory evidence: early-stage, so the film says "still being studied".
