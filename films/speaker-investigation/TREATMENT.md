# How a speaker is made: the paper trail (Visual Investigation, 9:16, ~60 s)

**Evidence:** US patent documents only (public domain). Every photo and video archive is blocked in this environment, so there is no footage or photography, and nothing is generated to stand in for it. The anatomy is reconstructed as a *schematic* cross-section, and each part is proven by a highlighted line on a real patent page.

**The clock:** dates. The readout changes form at each cut: 14 APR 1874 → 20 APR 1925 → 19 JUL 1944 → TODAY.

1. **Cold open on the finished schematic.** Five parts are circled with "?", and each patent answers one or two of them, ending on the evidence map. (Chosen: one question, answered source by source.)
